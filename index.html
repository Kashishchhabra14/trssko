Operations

How an order moves, end to end

From first inquiry to container tracking, every stage is confirmed in writing before the next one starts.

  • 01

    Specification & sample confirmation

    Buyer confirms grade, packaging and target price band; a paid sample is dispatched via air courier within 5 working days.

  • 02

    Proforma invoice & terms

    Incoterm, port of loading, payment terms (advance/LC) and lead time are fixed in a signed proforma invoice.

  • 03

    Production & pre-shipment inspection

    Batch is packed to spec; third-party inspection available on request before container stuffing.

  • 04

    Shipment & documentation

    Bill of lading, certificate of origin and compliance certificates are couriered ahead of vessel arrival for customs clearance.
