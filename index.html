01

Cyber Law Advisory

Legal counsel on cyber offences, digital evidence, online fraud, hacking, identity theft, and IT Act 2000 compliance for businesses and individuals.

Data Protection & DPDP Act

About TRSSKO

Company Profile

TRSSKO Fashion (OPC) Private Limited is a Meerut-based One Person Company registered under the Companies Act, 2013. Founded in 2023, we operate as an IEC-licensed merchant exporter across FMCG food products and fashion apparel — with a growing legal services vertical led by our founder-director.

Our vision is to carry premium Indian products and professional services to international markets while maintaining uncompromising quality, legal compliance, and authentic Indian identity in everything we do.
