Export Process

Your Product Clears Customs. Guaranteed.

Here's exactly how we handle every export shipment — from your first inquiry to delivery at your port. We answer the question every international buyer asks: "Will my product clear customs?"

01

RFQ & Quotation

Submit your product requirements, quantities, and destination country. We respond within 24 hours with a formal proforma invoice.

02

Quality Testing

All batches undergo quality inspection — moisture, colour grade, purity, and contaminant testing before packing.

03

Packaging & Labelling

Products are packed per your spec — retail, wholesale or bulk. Labels comply with UAE, UK, USA, Canada and Australian regulations.

04

Documentation

We prepare the full documentation package: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, CoA, Phytosanitary Certificate, and COO.

05

Customs Clearance

FSSAI-compliant exports processed through licensed customs brokers. We handle Indian export customs and advise on destination import procedures.

06

Delivery to Port

Shipment dispatched via sea (FCL/LCL) or air freight. Tracking details and original documents forwarded to buyer upon shipment.

📄

Documents We Provide

Commercial Invoice · Packing List · Bill of Lading · Airway Bill · Certificate of Analysis · Phytosanitary Certificate · Certificate of Origin · FSSAI Certificate · GST Invoice

🌍

Regulatory Compliance

We pre-check compliance with UAE MOIAT, UK FSA, USDA/FDA, CFIA (Canada), and DAFF (Australia) import requirements before every shipment.

🚢

Shipping Options

Sea freight (FCL/LCL) via major Indian ports · Air freight for urgent orders · DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) available for select destinations on request.

💬

Dedicated Support

A single point of contact for your order from RFQ to delivery. Email, WhatsApp and video call support available for all international buyers.
